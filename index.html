Year 01 · First Semester
Project 01

Foundation Studio 1: Place Exploration

Project overview

My first major project during the first semester was Foundation Studio 1.

The objective was to select a place, explore it and understand it beyond its physical appearance. We were expected to research, question and observe not just the artwork or architecture of the place, but also its environment, people and surroundings.

My approach

I explored the place through the perspective of a poet.

Instead of looking at the place only from a conventional design perspective, I tried to understand its atmosphere, emotions, stories and little details. I also attempted to communicate the place through the poet's point of view.

My process
  • Selecting and exploring a place.
  • Observing its surroundings and people.
  • Conducting visual and contextual research.
  • Questioning and documenting observations.
  • Developing a mood board.
  • Exploring a poet's perspective.
  • Communicating the experience visually.

What I learned

This project helped me understand that design begins with observation. It taught me to look beyond what is immediately visible and explore different ways of interpreting a place.

Project 03

Abstract Art Inspired by Elements

Project overview

My abstract art project inspired by elements is another creative exploration in my academic journey. It is about experimentation, visual thinking and creative expression.

Theme

This artwork captures the journey of hope. It tells the story of a butterfly's transition from dark to light.

On the left, the butterfly is in fractured surroundings, in hues of indigo and purple. As it moves, it steps towards the sun and its warm, fluid rays, a place that gives it the warmth and hope it needed to move forward and leave the darkness behind.

Gestalt principles

  • Figure and ground: the sun and the butterfly are the figure. The cool-toned geometric background is the ground.
  • Focal point: the sun, on the right.

Colour theme

I've used triadic colours to represent the dark and bright sides of the artwork.

  • Yellow
  • Blue
  • Red
Project 04

IRCTC: Research and Systems Design

"The Systemic Disconnect: A Systems Design Approach to Resolving Digital and Physical Friction in the IRCTC Ecosystem."

Project overview

This was a collaborative research project focused on understanding the problems passengers experience while interacting with IRCTC's digital platform and the physical railway station environment.

Our objective was to understand how digital difficulties and physical navigation problems affect the overall passenger experience.

Research methods

  • Survey of 127 users
  • On-site observations at KSR Bengaluru Railway Station
  • User research and interviews
  • Literature review
  • Cognitive Load Theory
  • Analysis of user satisfaction

Key research themes

  1. Spatial Navigation and Wayfinding Failure
  2. Cognitive Load and Temporal Stress
  3. Social Scaffolding and Reliance on Other People
  4. Accessibility and Inclusivity Gaps

My learning

The project helped me understand that design problems are not always limited to the interface of a product or application.

Sometimes the real problem exists within the larger system and the way different touchpoints interact with one another.

It helped me develop a more human-centred and research-oriented approach to design.

Major Business Communication project

SafeCommute

A Women-First Mobility Safety Platform.

"Know your route. Know your options."

SafeCommute is a women-first mobility safety platform designed to help women make safer and more confident commuting decisions.

The project focuses on understanding the safety concerns women experience while travelling and developing a platform that brings together route information, verified transport options and emergency support.

The objective is to make everyday mobility more informed, accessible and reassuring.

The problem

Women travelling alone, particularly at night, may experience uncertainty about routes, drivers, vehicle verification and journey safety.

SafeCommute explores how these concerns can be addressed through a connected mobility safety platform.

Three core pillars

01

Route Risk Insight

  • Street-level route-risk information.
  • Lighting and advisory maps.
  • Safe-haven directory.
  • Route comparisons.
02

Verified Fleet

  • Driver identity verification.
  • Vehicle verification.
  • Pre-boarding match codes.
  • Driver photograph checks.
03

Journey Monitoring

  • Real-time journey tracking.
  • Route deviation detection.
  • Trusted-contact journey sharing.
  • Emergency SOS support.
